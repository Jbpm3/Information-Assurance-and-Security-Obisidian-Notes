## 10.3.2 Hardware-Based and Software-Based Technologies

**Software firewalls** - control remote access to a system

**Network and port scanners** - discover and monitor open ports on a host or server

**Protocol analyzers** - also known as *signature analyzers*. devices that collect and examine network traffic

**Vulnerability scanners** - programs designed to assess weaknesses on computers or networks

**Host-based intrusion detection systems (IDS)** - These examine activity on host systems only
