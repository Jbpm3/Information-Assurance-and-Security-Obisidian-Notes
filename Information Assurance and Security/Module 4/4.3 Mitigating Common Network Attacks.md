## 4.3.3 Mitigating Worms
![[Mitigating Worms.png]]

**Containment** - limits the spread of a worm infection to areas of the network already affected
**Inoculation** - runs parallel to or subsequent to the containment phase.
		- all uninfected systems are patched with the appropriate vendor patch
		- deprives the worm of any available targets
**Quarantine** - tracks down and identifies machines within the contained areas and disconnecting, blocking, or removing them
**Treatment** - actively disinfects infected systems
