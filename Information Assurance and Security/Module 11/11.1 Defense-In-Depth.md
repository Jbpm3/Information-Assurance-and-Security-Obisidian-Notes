## 11.1.1 Assets, Vulnerabilities, Threats
**Assets** - anything of value that must be protected including servers, infrastructure devices, end devices, and data
**Vulnerabilities** - weakness in a system or its design that could be exploited by a threat actor
**Threats** - potential danger to an asset

---
## 11.1.2 Identify Assets
*The collection of all the devices and information owned or managed by the organization are assets.*

*Asset management consists of inventorying all assets, and then developing and implementing policies and procedures to protect them.*

---
## 11.1.4 Asset Classification
asset identification category:
- Information assets
- Software assets
- Physical assets
- Services
