## 2.1.5 Data Loss
*Data* is likely to be an organization's most valuable asset

Data loss can result in:
- Brand damage and loss of reputation
- Loss of competitive advantage
- Loss of customers
- Loss of revenue
- Litigation/legal action that results in fines and civil penalties
- Significant cost and effort to notify affected parties and recover from the breach

**Common Data Loss Vectors:**
- Email/Social Networking
- Unencrypted devices
- Cloud Storage Devices
- Removable Media
- Hard Copy
- Improper Access Control
